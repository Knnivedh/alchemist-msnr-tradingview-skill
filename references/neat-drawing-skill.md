## 0. The one-sentence skill

**One chart, one idea, one zone, thin black lines, labels in empty space — and every confirmation is one drawn mark (a dot, a tick, an X), never a paragraph.**

## 2. Visual grammar — the complete vocabulary

| Mark | Means | How to draw it |
|---|---|---|
| Thin black horizontal line | A level (a price) | 1 px solid, full width or from its swing to the right edge |
| Stepped black line (right angle) | A swing high/low carried right | Vertical drop from the swing, then horizontal — the step shows which swing the level came from |
| Dotted horizontal line | A reference / target level | Thin dotted, full width, price tag on the axis |
| Dashed horizontal line | A measured / order level | Dashed; red for SELL/SL, green for TP, brown/orange for ladder levels |
| Dashed diagonal | The measured move / trendline | Thin dashed, swing high → swing low (or low → high) |
| Yellow translucent rectangle | A zone (imbalance / profile / QML / order block) | Fill only, ~20–30% opacity, edges = defining candles' wicks/bodies, no text inside |
| Orange translucent rectangle | A demand / IMB zone at the lows (same meaning as yellow — colour varies by page, not by rule) | Same as yellow; do not assign it a different meaning |
| Teal translucent rectangle | A projection box (top = range high, bottom = ERL) | Spans future bars, not past price action |
| Pink / cyan vertical bar | A session/time bar | Thin vertical rectangle, one candle wide (construction unstated — do not invent a session) |
| Red dot | An exact touch — the dot, not the line, is the level | One red dot on the touching candle, per level |
| Green ✓ in a white box | Validation confirmed | Small box + green tick, leader line to the validated level |
| Black X | A failed / invalidated level | One small X at the swing — replaces a paragraph |
| Blue ↑ / red ↓ arrows | The swing low (entry-side) / swing high (rejection-side) | At the two ends of the dashed diagonal (mobile pages) |
| Red ▼ / blue ◆ | The diagonal's top anchor / bottom anchor | Red ▼ at the high, blue ◆ at the low |
| 💡 (lightbulb) | The shift moment | After MSS / SHIFT — the moment structure turned |
| Bracket `[ ... ]` | A structure event (BOS / MSS / IDM) | Short horizontal bracket at the swing break, label at its end |
| 4-segment colour bar (grey/red/green/blue) | The quarterly / DAILY CIRCLE bar | Short horizontal bar below the price action; dot under the current segment (meaning unstated — do not map it to clock times) |

**Zone vs level vs touch (the three-way grammar):** a zone is where the entry/decision is taken *inside an area* (the yellow rectangle); a level is where a break is *measured* (BOS/MSS judged by a line being crossed); a dot is where a touch is *recorded* (the exact candle). The PDF never states this in words — it is consistent across every page.

## 4. The measurement spec (points, %, bars, duration)

The book's proof format — the cleanest template in the whole PDF is the **p.39 phone screenshot**. Copy this format first.

**Template (p.39, H12):**
- 5 dashed horizontal lines at the priced levels, 4 red-flag BUY markers on the left, 1 light-blue translucent measurement rectangle spanning the whole move, 1 black stats box.
- Stats box, line 1: `737484 points (+18.70%, +737.484)` — i.e. `points (% , ±points)`.
- Stats box, line 2: `56 bars, 4w` — i.e. `bars, duration`.
- **Verified arithmetic:** 4,681.360 − 3,943.876 = 737.484 ✓; 737.484 / 3,943.876 = 18.70% ✓.
- **Reverse move (p.41):** `687471 points (-14.85%, -687.471)` / `78 bars, 5w 4d`; 4,627.899 − 3,940.428 = 687.471 ✓; 687.471 / 4,627.899 = 14.85% ✓.
- **Rules:** the rectangle measures a *completed* move — it is not a trading zone. Dashed lines are measured levels, not key levels. The lines are dashed (not solid) because they are *measured*, not confirmed. White space dominates — the chart occupies the middle, top and bottom are empty.

**Ladder spec (pp.39, 43):**
- BUY ladder (p.39): 4 levels — 4,452.191 / 4,315.232 / 3,976.266 / 3,961.134 (dashed, red-flag markers).
- SELL ladder (p.43): 7 levels — 5,415.461 / 5,375.580 / 4,888.984 / 4,769.709 / 4,695.401 / 4,491.899 / 4,396.553 (dashed; 2 red, 5 brown/black — distinction unexplained, do not assume).
- Levels are spaced ~40–200 points apart, denser at the top. No stop, no target, no lot size, no outcome is shown for a ladder — a ladder is a *set of levels*, not a trade.

## 5. The phone-screenshot template (p.39 as the cleanest)

Use this as the default layout for any measured move:

1. Black/white candles, no indicators, no coloured overlays.
2. Dashed horizontals at each priced level, full width, price tag in a coloured box on the axis (orange for ladder levels, black for the measurement bounds).
3. Red-flag markers at the levels that were entries (BUY/SELL + flag, one per level).
4. One translucent measurement rectangle (light blue, filled) spanning the whole move, OR one dashed outline rectangle (p.41) — pick one, not both.
5. One black stats box: line 1 `points (% , ±points)`, line 2 `bars, duration`.
6. Nothing else. No text boxes, no labels on the chart, no legend.
7. Header: instrument + TF. Footer: date + instrument | TF. Watermark: your own tag, diagonal, light.

## 6. Step-by-step recipe — drawing one trade in TradingView

### A. The ladder trade (gold, pp.25–27) — 5 steps

1. **D1 — find the PROFILE.** Open the Daily chart. Find the biggest daily candle (the range-defining candle). Highlight it with a yellow translucent rectangle covering its full height (body + wicks). Mark the ERL below the range: one thin red horizontal line at the range low, dotted extension to the right. Print the ladder on the chart, top-left, three lines: `D1 > PROFILE / H1 > MINORITY + MSS / H4 > MAJORITY + CONFIRM`. Labels so far: 3. Stop here — do not add structure yet.
2. **H4 — wait for HIT IMB.** Open the 240M chart. Draw the majority IMB zone: one wide yellow translucent rectangle at the top of the prior range. Mark the MSS line: one thin red horizontal at the swing high. Wait for price to rally and wick into the zone. When the wick touches it, write `HIT IMB` with a leader line to the touching wick. That one wick touch is the validation — no second tap is required by the book.
3. **H1 — take the MSS, then validate.** Open the 60M chart. Mark the MSS line at the equivalent swing (one thin red horizontal). Wait for the take (the break). Then wait for validation: price returning to / holding a small yellow zone after the take. When it holds, add a green ✓ in a small white box with a leader line to the high. Labels so far on this chart: MSS, VALIDATION AFTER TAKE MSS, ✓. Stop.
4. **Write the direction.** On the H4 chart, write `TARGET MAJORITY IMB TO LIQ` with leader lines to the ERL. Direction is now: imbalance → liquidity (short into the zone, target the lows). No entry price, no stop, no target number is given by the book — if you add one, label it as yours, not the book's.
5. **Record it.** Copy the Trade 16 journal card (p.20): pair, direction, TF chain, confluences (CRT | CLEAR LIQUDITAS | NY AM), narrative (IMB TO LIQ), open/close dates, result (Win/Lose). The card carries the reasoning — the chart does not.

### B. The order (silver, pp.13–16) — 4 steps

1. **D1 analysis:** mark structure — IDM → BOS → MSS (thin black brackets). Mark the liquidity pool above (thin red horizontal, LIQUIDITY) and the +TS sweep high (red dot on the highest wick). Note `CLEAR VALIDASI` when price clears the validation level (thin black line + dot at its end).
2. **Place the order:** three red dashed horizontals for the SELL LIMIT zone (entry zone = the band, not a line), SL as the top dashed line above the zone, TP as one green dotted horizontal at the swing low. Price tags in coloured boxes on the axis (red for SL/entry, green for TP).
3. **Draw the diagonal:** one thin black dashed line from the swing high (red ▼) to the swing low (blue ◆). Watch for rejections on it — a second red ▼ marks a retest (p.17).
4. **Prove the sequence:** screenshot the limit placed (p.14) → screenshot the tap-and-reject (p.16: wick touches the entry, long red candle follows, current price tagged). Two screenshots, one new fact each.

### C. The master reference layer (pp.29/31–35) — add only when needed

1. **Fib circle:** 2–3 very thin black arcs, page-spanning, centre far off-page. No fan, no colours, no 11 levels. The STDV numbers (2 / 2.5 / 3 / 3.5 / 4 / 4.5 / 5 / 5.5 / 6) sit *on* the arcs — the scale and the circle are one object.
2. **Quarterly legend:** the Q1–Q4 text block in the empty lower half of the chart, never on candles. Copy the wording from §7 of the merged report — and flag the Q3/Q4 contradictions (see §8 below) rather than picking one gloss silently.
3. **Structure:** BOS brackets at each break, red dots at each touch, X marks at each failed level.
4. **Text blocks:** Premium Zone, High Risk Confirmation, Draw on Liquidity / Quarterly Theory — placed in empty space, in the same three blocks every time. The blocks never change; only the chart does. Memorise the three blocks, then read the chart.
5. **Label the failure:** FAILURE at a peak that did not hold, INVERSION where structure inverted, LAST LIQUIDITY at the final pool, ERL TAKEN once the target is hit.

### D. The trendline fan (pp.41–42, 44–45) — cap at 5 lines

1. Pick one swing low as the convergence point.
2. Draw one dashed diagonal from each successive swing high to that low. Cap at **~5 lines** — beyond that the fan becomes unreadable (p.45 is the failure case: 6+ overlapping lines).
3. Mark the anchors: red down-arrows at the highs, blue up-arrow at the low. Small red/blue triangle markers at the swing points.
4. No prices are needed to draw the fan — but without prices it cannot be replicated exactly. If the price axis is cropped, say so; do not guess the levels.

## 8. Honest limits — what this skill sheet cannot give you

- **No thresholds:** what counts as a "hit", a "validation", a "shift", a "rejection" is never defined numerically. One wick touch is the only drawn rule (HIT IMB, p.27). Everything else needs a threshold *you* choose and write down.
- **No prices on most charts:** the 240M master pages carry no dollar prices — the right axis is the Fib scale (2–5.5). Only the phone pages (pp.9, 14–16, 39, 41, 43) give priced levels. A neat drawing that cannot be re-placed from its own labels is neat but not reproducible.
- **No PROFILE / CRT selection rule:** "largest candle" is the visible rule; no size threshold (range vs ATR, vs prior candles). Same gap as the Alchemist CRT candle.
- **Fib / STDV unstated:** no centre, radius, ratios or anchors for the circle; "+2-2.5" is a citation, not a formula; no price-to-STDV mapping.
- **No entry trigger:** no M15 chart anywhere; no trigger rule for the lowest timeframe. No stop distance, no lot size, no risk %, no R:R, no session times (NY AM is named, no clock times).
- **Contradictions to flag, not resolve:** Q3 "START IN PREMIUM AREA" vs "START IN DISCOUNT"; Q4 "(DISCOUNT ZONE)" + "EXPENSIVE PRICE / SUITABLE FOR BUY" vs "(PREMIUM ZONE)"; two ladders (MN/W1/D1 vs D1/H1/H4); AMDX/XAMD vs A-M-D/M-A-D; tick-scale direction flips (p.5 vs pp.8/12); three-plus unreconciled lows for the XAGUSD sell (54.776 / 54.604 / 52.707 / 52.758 / 52.085).
- **Undefined abbreviations — do not expand:** IOFED, +TS, BOSa, BOSm, BOSs, C.A, SSS, FS, IQFBD, EQE, BOI, L.T4/L.T8, CLA, TS^, +7B^, TOFED, C.V, "60 IDR", masked text. "INDOCEMENT" and "STRUKTUR" are the author's spellings — quote as written.
- **No performance evidence:** one journal card marked Win, one P&L banner (+20,462.50 USD, no order ticket), two measured moves. No backtest, no win rate, no sample size, no losing example with numbers. One winning example is not a system.
