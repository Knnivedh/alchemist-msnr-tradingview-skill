## Headline verdict
This is a set of annotated example screenshots, not a procedure. 34 pages hold only about 10-12 distinct charts: pp.3-8 are one XAUUSD chart shown six times, pp.10-16 are one US30 Quarterly chart shown seven times, pp.19-24 are one XAUUSD 30m chart repeated and zoomed, pp.20-21 are byte-identical duplicates. The core drawing primitive is Candle Range Theory (CRT) + Consequent Encroachment (CE): take one reference candle, its high = CRT/.HIGH, its low = CRT/.LOW, the 50% midpoint = CE. Everything else hangs off that range: External Range Liquidity (ERL) at the extremes, Internal Range Liquidity (IRL) / FVG inside it, premium above 50% / discount below 50%, buy below 50% / sell above 50%.
The one worked gold example is a buy in discount: HTF APEX CE ~4,333.78 (pp.3-8, 17-20 Aug 2026) -> M5 buy ~4,336.09 on 19 Aug -> TP1 4,445.19 touched, TP2 4,449.71 untouched (p.9). A second, longer hold is shown on p.27: XAUUSD M30 buy 4,335.23 on 19 Aug 08:30, high 4,696.86, +361.63 points / +8.34% over 174 bars to 25 Aug — no stop, exit, lot size, or dollar P&L shown.
Replication verdict: an agent can replicate the vocabulary, the CE / 50% equilibrium arithmetic, and the static labelling (BOS/MSS/CISD/FVG once thresholds are assumed). It CANNOT replicate the exact drawing end to end from this PDF alone, because the decisions that make the drawing specific — which candle is the CRT candle, what counts as a rejection, which prior highs/lows qualify as key levels, how a zone's edges are set, what the Fib-circle construction is, and when/how to drop from HTF to LTF — are never defined.

## How each zone is captured
A zone is a filled rectangle/band with height; a level is a single line. Zones in the book: FVG+IRL grey (p.2), RESIS/.T DAILY grey (p.3), APEX CE blue (pp.5, 19), light-blue rectangle under QML (pp.10-12), vertical pink/blue POOL LIQUIDITY bar (pp.10-12), blue diagonal Accumulation parallelogram (pp.13-16, tilted, hugging the sideways range, bottom edge = OCL/accumulation lows), red top band ~52,252-52,263 (pp.15-16), grey Key-Level box (p.19), yellow Accumulation parallelogram (p.19), purple ranging box (p.23).
Identification is by confluence, in the order the pages present it: (1) it is the CE/50% of a defined range or leg; (2) it coincides with a prior structure point (FVG, daily resistance, prior support); (3) a trendline or STDV projection meets price there; (4) it sits in discount (below the range 50%) for a buy. Key levels are defined (p.15, p.19) as "forked [formed] from liquidity, previous high/low, OB, FVG and market structure" — circular, no selection rule.
Validation is only structural, never numeric: a zone is treated as valid when price taps it and then a BOS / SHIFT follows (the V-recovery off APEX CE 4,333.71 -> 4,449.27 is the one worked example). No tap count, no wick-vs-body rule, no zone-width rule, no time-in-zone rule, no invalidation rule. A zone is where entry is taken inside a band on rejection; a level is where a break is measured (BOS/SHIFT/CISD judged by a line being crossed). The PDF never states this distinction in words — it is inferred from how the two are drawn. In pp.26-34 there are almost no zones at all — everything is a line + red circle, and the 50% equilibrium area has no drawn boundaries.

## Drawing style end to end — the visual workflow (reconstructed, not numbered in the PDF)
1. Open the HTF chart and mark the reference candle's high/low (CRT).
2. Draw the CE at 50% of that candle; shade the FVG+IRL zone inside the range.
3. Extend the range to ERL at the extremes; mark buy-side and sell-side liquidity pools.
4. Draw the premium/discount split (50% equilibrium of High-Low); decide buy-below-50% / sell-above-50%.
5. Mark structure: BOS at each swing break, SHIFT (MSS) where direction changes, CISD at the shift origin; bracket accumulation and inducement legs; mark QML and SINGLE L/Q at the top.
6. Mark daily resistance and SBR; identify the APEX CE where the trendline meets the CE in discount after a sell-side sweep (turtle soup below). Draw it as a blue zone, label it the HTF key rejection first-entry level.
7. Add context layers: STDV projection, Q2/.M level, Quarterly Theory quarter, MACRO / 90M / DAILY CIRCLE bars.
8. Drop to M5/M1: draw entry line at the APEX price, SL line, TP lines, and the LTF trendline; wait for price to react at the level and rally.
9. Entry models (pp.29-34): Classic V support buy / Classic A resistance sell (line through the extreme, later candle touches it, red circle); CE entry at 25% / 50% / 75% of the V/A (no rule for which %); RBS/SBR flip (break, then retest holds); QML shoulder entry; OCL entry (line at the open/close of the displacement candle).
10. Manage by rejection and by partials/liquidity targets: entry on HTF key-level rejection (wick reaction), explicitly high-risk / early; targets are the opposite liquidity (ERL, Swing Liquidity Targets N1/N2).
Steps 1-9 are the order the drawings imply. The PDF itself contains no numbered steps and no prose procedure — this sequence is an inference from label layering, and the trigger (what exactly counts as "rejection") is undefined.
